A → Z of the Interactive Web

A field guide, catalogued alphabetically: one working specimen of browser behaviour for every letter, from Animate to Zoom.

Begin at A ↓
    A

    Animate /ˈæn.ɪ.meɪt/

    interactive · verb

    To set a still shape in motion at the moment someone asks for it, rather than the moment the page loads.

    Click the mark. Each press restarts the animation from zero.

    B

    Background /ˈbæk.graʊnd/

    interactive · noun

    A field of small points, quietly connecting to whichever ones are nearest, and to your cursor when it comes close.

    Move your mouse over the panel.

    C

    Customize /ˈkʌs.tə.maɪz/

    interactive · verb

    To let the reader pick the one colour that speaks for the whole page, and watch every accent obey it at once.

    D

    Drag /dræɡ/

    interactive · verb

    To pick a line out of its place and set it down somewhere truer, using nothing but the pointer.

    Reorder the specimens by dragging them.

    F

    Form /fɔːrm/

    interactive · noun

    A pair of fields that check their own work — an address for shape, a password for strength — before either is accepted.

    G

    Gallery /ˈɡæl.ər.i/

    interactive · noun

    A row of plates, viewed one at a time, each announced quietly at the bottom of its own frame.

    H

    Hover /ˈhʌv.ər/

    interactive · verb

    To linger over a label just long enough that it explains itself, and to vanish the instant you look away.

    Wild rose Bat Stalactite Tooltip, itself

    JSON /ˈdʒeɪ.sɒn/

    interactive · noun

    A small store of facts, kept as plain text, drawn from at random whenever the reader asks for one more.

    Press the button for a field note.
    K

    Keyboard /ˈkiː.bɔːrd/

    interactive · noun

    A second way in, for hands that never leave the keys — every letter here a shortcut to its own entry.

    L

    Local storage /ˈloʊ.kəl ˈstɔːr.ɪdʒ/

    interactive · noun

    A notebook kept in the browser itself — closed, reopened, and still holding exactly what was written in it.

      M

      interactive · adjective

      N

      interactive · verb

      O

      Online /ˈɒn.laɪn/

      interactive · adjective

      A single fact the browser already knows about the room you're in — whether it is, right now, connected to anything at all.
